The Problem

Current Pain Points in Local Issuances

1. Disconnected Offices

  • Citizens visit Barangay and City Hall separately
  • Manual paper endorsements get misplaced
  • Zero cross-agency data interoperability

2. Slow Turnaround

  • 2–5 days average queuing & processing
  • ARTA red-tape non-compliance risks
  • No real-time tracking for citizens

3. Fraud & Verification Risks

  • Tampered paper certificates & fake clearances
  • No live digital verification ledger
  • Lack of PhilSys identity cross-matching

Challenge: Transforming multi-day bureaucratic friction into sub-2-hour digital delivery.

The Solution

Dual-Tier Document Architecture

Two specialized workflows under one unified administration portal:

📄 Tab 1: Barangay Clearances

  • Barangay Clearance for Employment & Postal ID
  • Certificate of Indigency (DSWD / Medical)
  • Certificate of Residency (Bank / Court Proof)
  • First-Time Jobseeker (RA 11261 Fee Exemption)
  • Barangay Business Clearance

🏛️ Tab 2: LGU Endorsements & Permits

  • Mayor's / Business Permit Endorsement
  • Building Permit & Locational Clearance
  • City Zoning & Land Use Endorsement
  • City Health & Sanitation Permit
  • Real Property Tax (RPT) Assessor Clearance

All records seamlessly integrated with resident identity database.

Statutory Framework

Philippine Legal & Regulatory Compliance

⚖️ RA 11032 (Ease of Doing Business)

  • Automated SLA timers tracking 3-day turnaround limits
  • Zero-contact online processing capabilities

🎓 RA 11261 (First-Time Jobseekers Act)

  • Automatic ₱0.00 fee waiver for first-time job applicants
  • One-touch Oath of Undertaking encoding

🆔 PhilSys (RA 11055) Integration

  • Instant citizen PCN identity verification
  • Duplicate prevention across households

🔒 RA 10173 (Data Privacy Act)

  • Masked public QR verification (initials only)
  • Role-based access control (PB, Secretary, Treasurer)

100% compliant with Philippine national administrative standards.
